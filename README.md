## 🌍 The Mission: Why SolarWise Exists

Residential solar is the single most actionable climate transition mechanism available to urban and suburban homeowners today. However, millions of households hesitate or fail to adopt clean energy due to **information asymmetry, predatory over-sizing by sales-driven contractors, and opaque financial payback models**.

**SolarWise** solves this bottleneck by providing homeowners with an **honest, un-biased, and data-driven feasibility consultant**. Rather than treating solar as a one-size-fits-all commodity, SolarWise evaluates **the exact physical and technical realities of each homeowner's living environment**:
- Usable rooftop geometry, shading profiles, and structural orientation.
- Actual historical electricity consumption extracted straight from utility bills.
- Slab-wise tariff structures, net-metering caps, and DISCOM interconnection rules.
- Living space constraints and future decarbonization goals (EV charging, induction cooking, heat pumps).

Most importantly, SolarWise is **radically honest**: if your roof is too shaded, your electricity consumption is too low to amortize capital costs, or your utility's buyback rate makes installation economically unviable, the platform explicitly tells you **"Not Recommended"** or advises a right-sized partial setup rather than pushing an unnecessary investment.

### 1. ☀️ Renewables: Intelligent Feasibility for Your Living Environment

Solar energy generation is strictly dictated by local micro-climates, roof morphology, and urban layouts. SolarWise tailors renewable deployment to individual homes:

- **Living Environment Adaptation**:
  - **Flat Concrete Terraces**: Optimal seasonal tilt (15°–25° south-facing) with wind ballast and walking perimeter clearances.
  - **Sloped & Metal Roofs**: Flush-mounted coplanar arrays accounting for roof structural pitch and azimuth azimuth losses.
  - **Urban Suburban Homes**: Multi-pitch tracing that isolates unshaded roof sectors from adjoining buildings and trees.
- **2D Canvas Roof Digital Planner**: Homeowners visually trace their roof perimeter, set physical dimensions, mark obstacles (water overhead tanks, AC compressors, stairwells, vents), and tile solar modules with automated safety setbacks.
- **Renewable Hardware Guides**:
  - **Panels**: High-efficiency N-type TOPCon and Mono-PERC modules (450Wp–550Wp) with 30-year performance warranties.
  - **Inverters**: String and hybrid MPPT inverters sized with 1.15–1.25 DC/AC clipping ratios.
  - **Storage**: Safe Lithium Iron Phosphate (LFP / $\text{LiFePO}_4$) battery systems for nocturnal independence and outage resilience.
- **Regional Subsidy & Policy Guides**: Direct integration with the **PM Surya Ghar: Muft Bijli Yojana** policy framework (calculating up to ₹78,000 CFA central subsidy and state top-ups) plus an independent, non-sponsored directory of MNRE/DISCOM-empanelled installers.

### 2. 📉 Emission Tracking & Decarbonization Calculators

SolarWise turns complex climate data into tangible, verified environmental indicators that empower homeowners to track and accelerate their personal emissions reduction:

- **25-Year Lifecycle Carbon Displacement Engine**:
  - Uses regional grid carbon emission factors (e.g. India CEA baseline: $\sim 0.82\text{ kg } \text{CO}_2/\text{kWh}$) to calculate the exact carbon offset generated by your rooftop array over its full 25-year operational lifecycle.
  - Accounts for module degradation curves (0.55%/year) to prevent overstated environmental gains.
- **Intuitive Real-World Environmental Equivalents**:
  - **Mature Trees Planted**: Equivalent forest carbon sequestration over 10 and 25 years.
  - **Passenger Vehicle Kilometers Avoided**: Real-world combustion vehicle emissions neutralized.
  - **Barrels of Oil & Coal Saved**: Concrete metric tonnes of fossil fuels displaced from thermal power plants.
- **Actionable Ways to Reduce Emissions (Home Decarbonization Roadmap)**:
  - **Daytime Load Shifting**: Guidance on shifting high-draw loads (washing machines, dishwashers, pool pumps, water heaters) into the solar generation window (10:00 AM – 3:00 PM) to boost clean self-consumption from 35% to 65%+.
  - **Electrification Integration**: Modeling future demand additions like Electric Vehicles (EVs) and heat pumps, turning rooftop solar into a localized fueling station.

### 3. ⚡ Grid Metrics & Decentralized Infrastructure Analytics

Decentralized rooftop solar transforms passive energy consumers into active clean-energy "prosumers", directly stabilizing local electrical grids:

- **24-Hour Diurnal Load & Generation Curves**:
  - Visualizing the synchronization between solar irradiance bell curves and household consumption spikes.
  - Detailed split between direct solar self-consumption, battery charging, and clean grid feed-in exports.
- **Marginal Avoided Costs vs. Feed-In Compensation**:
  - Calculates true savings against tiered slab utility tariffs (avoiding high-cost peak grid slabs first).
  - Explicitly accounts for non-parity net-metering buyback rates (e.g. ₹2.25/kWh export credit vs ₹7.50/kWh consumption cost) to prevent inflated contractor claims.
- **Local Air Quality & Grid Relief Impact**:
  - **Substation Thermal Relief**: Generating clean electricity at the point of consumption relieves thermal stress on local distribution transformers during summer heatwaves.
  - **Transmission Loss Avoidance**: Eliminates the 15%–22% aggregate technical and commercial (AT&C) transmission losses incurred when importing electricity from distant coal stations.
  - **Airshed Depollution**: Displacing coal combustion directly reduces local emissions of Particulate Matter ($\text{PM}_{2.5}$, $\text{PM}_{10}$), Sulfur Dioxide ($\text{SO}_2$), and Nitrogen Oxides ($\text{NO}_x$).

## 💡 Dual User Assessment Experience

To make clean energy accessible to all homeowners regardless of their technical background, SolarWise provides two fully synchronized assessment experiences:

1. **Guided View (Conversational Consultant)**:
   - Step-by-step conversational dialogue tailored for everyday homeowners.
   - Smart Bill OCR: Upload any electricity bill image or PDF to automatically extract monthly units, consumer numbers, and DISCOM rate cards.
   - Plain-English explanations and contextual jargon tooltips for complex engineering terms (e.g., Performance Ratio, Net Metering, Degradation).
2. **Detailed View (Technical & Multi-Metric Stepper)**:
   - Complete 7-step engineering stepper for technical users, engineers, and energy auditors.
   - Full visibility into DC/AC ratios, tilt/azimuth sensitivity curves, 25-year cumulative cash flows, Net Present Value (NPV), Levelized Cost of Electricity (LCOE), and Internal Rate of Return (IRR).
   - **Zero-Loss Two-Way Sync**: Seamlessly toggle between Guided and Detailed views at any stage with instant bi-directional state synchronization.

## 🚀 Local Development Setup

### 1. Prerequisites
- **Node.js**: `20.x` or higher
- **npm**: `10.x` or higher

### 2. Installation
```bash
# Clone the repository
git clone https://github.com/3bin-05/SolarWise.git
cd SolarWise

# Install dependencies
npm ci
```

### 3. Environment Configuration
Create a `.env.local` file from the provided example:
```bash
cp .env.example .env.local
```
Configure your environment variables:
```env
VITE_API_BASE_URL=http://localhost:8000/api/v1
VITE_APP_ENV=development
```

### 4. Start Development Server
```bash
npm run dev
```
Open [http://localhost:5173](http://localhost:5173) in your browser.

### 5. Code Quality & Testing Suite
```bash
# Run Vitest unit & integration test suites
npm run test:run

# Execute TypeScript type checking
npm run typecheck

# Run fast Oxlint static analysis
npm run lint

# Compile production build
npm run build
```
